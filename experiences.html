Infinity pool

Take a refreshing swim beside Secret Lake, with open water and mountain views stretching beyond the pool. The lake-bank setting invites you to slow down and enjoy the scenery.

Spend time on the poolside deck between swims, or combine your visit with a meal at our restaurant. Contact our team before arriving to ask about pool access and plan your day.

Secret Lake

A 1.2m-wide bridge leads to three wooden huts with grass roofs on the water. Follow the walkway for a closer view of the lake and the hills beyond its shores.

Choose a lakeside setting for time with family or friends, and enjoy the changing light across the water. Ask our team about reserving a hut for your visit or meal.

Pepper farms

Make Secret Lake House your base for exploring Kampot's pepper-growing countryside. Nearby farm names include La Plantation, Bo Tree Farm, Starling Farm, Sindora, and The Pepperhill.

Combine a countryside outing with time beside the lake and a meal on your return. Check directly with your chosen farm for opening hours, tour availability, and any booking requirements.

Kampot access

Secret Lake House is around 12km from Kampot, on Road 1331 beside Secret Lake. Our countryside location offers a change of pace from town for a day visit or an overnight stay.

Use our Google Maps link to plan your journey, and contact the team if you need help finding us. Share your arrival plans when booking a room, dining hut, or restaurant table.

Pepper Trail & Rural Exploration

Tucked right along Kampot's iconic culinary route, Secret Lake House serves as an effortless base camp for exploring the region's world-renowned organic pepper farms.

The surrounding countryside invites slow-paced adventure, where winding red-dirt roads lead past traditional wooden stilt houses, emerald rice paddies, and peaceful pastoral scenes. Whether you set off by motorbike or bicycle, the journey through the rural landscape is as rich and sensory as the destination itself, offering an authentic look into the agricultural heartbeat of southern Cambodia.

Lakefront Serenity & Culinary Discoveries

After a morning spent sampling fresh green peppercorns and learning the centuries-old harvesting methods of local growers, the lake offers a quiet retreat to decompress. Secret Lake House captures the best of both worlds: immediate access to the farm-to-table trails and a front-row seat to still, open waters.

Guests can return from their countryside excursions to unwind on the waterfront deck, watch the afternoon light shift across the hills, and savor regional dishes infused with the very spices cultivated just down the road.
