Stay in a traditional Khmer House at Secret Lake House, with three rooms arranged across two floors for guests visiting the lake and countryside around Kampot.

The first floor has two Suite Rooms, each with one double bed. These rooms offer an option for couples or guests who prefer a double-bed arrangement.

On the ground floor, the Twin Deluxe Room provides an alternative for guests who prefer separate beds. Let our team know your preferred room when enquiring.

The house bathrooms combine warm wood, stone basins, and walk-in showers. After exploring the surrounding countryside, return to the house to unwind.

Contact us with your dates and group size to ask about individual rooms or booking the full Khmer House. Our team will confirm availability, current rates, and the arrangement that suits your stay.

Bungalow Family Room

The Bungalow Family Room brings families together in one shared room at Secret Lake House, providing a base for a relaxed visit to the lake and Kampot countryside.

The room is arranged with one double bed and one bunk bed, allowing family members to share a room while having separate sleeping spaces.

Spend your visit enjoying time together by the lake or exploring the nearby countryside. The bungalow gives your family a place to return to between outings.

Our restaurant is open daily from 7:00am to 9:00pm. Dinner requires an advance booking, so please let our team know if you would like to dine during your stay.

Send us your travel dates and the number of adults and children travelling with you. We will confirm availability, the room rate, and whether the sleeping arrangement meets your family's needs.

The Family Studio Room offers a shared bungalow stay at Secret Lake House for families, friends, or small groups travelling together.

With two bunk beds, the room provides separate sleeping spaces while keeping everyone together in one room.

Make it your base for time by the lake and exploring the countryside around Kampot, then return to relax after the day's outings.

Meals can be enjoyed at our restaurant, open daily from 7:00am to 9:00pm. Please book dinner in advance so our team can prepare for your visit.

Contact us with your travel dates and the number of adults and children in your group. Our team will confirm availability, the room rate, and whether the arrangement suits your stay.
